About Blue Door Café & Bakes

Blue Door Café & Bakes was founded with a love for artisanal European baking and rich, velvety specialty roasts. From flaky breakfast pastries and fluffy banana blueberry pancakes to decadent Lotus Biscoff cheesecakes, every recipe is made from scratch with authentic butter, fine chocolate, and pure passion.
